## Annie's few minutes

**1. She arrives — no wall in front of her.**
Annie types `123 Cherry St` into our site. No login, no email, no credit card. She just wants to see if a backyard home is possible.
*Why it matters: removing the sign-up wall is the single biggest lever on top-of-funnel conversion. She gets value before we ask for anything.*

**2. Her neighborhood swoops into view.**
The screen lifts from a flat map into a 3D view of her actual street — her real roof, her real trees, the mountains behind. It feels like the "fly-in" from a premium map app, but it's *her house.*
*Why it matters: this is the emotional hook. People don't connect to a price calculator; they connect to their own home. This is the "wow" that makes her keep going.*

**3. The buildable space lights up.**
A glowing outline rises out of her backyard showing exactly where a new home could legally sit — set back the right distance from her house and her fences.
*Why it matters: she instantly sees "yes, this is real and it fits," which replaces months of uncertainty and phone calls. Confidence early = fewer drop-offs.*

**4. A matched home appears, in her yard, at true size.**
A recommended home model fades in inside that space — to scale, next to her real patio and trees. She can spin the view and walk around it. Labels show the honest distances ("12 feet from the fence"). She sees the full price up front, broken into plain buckets — the home itself, the site work, and the permits — each with a one-line "here's why."
*Why it matters: her #1 fear is "the price will balloon." Showing an honest, all-in number with nothing hidden is what earns the trust to move forward — and the price on screen lets her self-qualify on affordability without us asking.*

**5. She can see it at different times of day.**
A simple slider lets her watch how the new home's shadow falls across her yard at 9am, noon, and 5pm — useful if she's worried about her garden or her neighbor.
*Why it matters: it answers a real, specific worry no brochure ever addresses, which deepens trust and makes the tool feel genuinely helpful, not salesy.*

**6. "Start My Project" — interest, not commitment.**
When she's excited, one button lets her raise her hand. She gives an email and an optional note; a real person (her concierge) reaches out within a day. Still no payment.
*Why it matters: this is the lead-capture moment, captured at peak excitement rather than forced upfront. It's also where she's naturally invited to save her report with a free account.*

**7. She makes it hers.**
She can dress the home like online shopping — siding color, roof style, add a deck — and step "inside" to choose flooring, the kitchen, and any accessibility features (a real draw for the "building this for my parents" buyer). As she chooses, the price updates live, and the home stays sitting in her actual backyard. If she drags it somewhere it wouldn't be allowed, it gently turns red and won't let her.
*Why it matters: this is the upsell and the deepening of commitment. Every choice she makes is one more reason to follow through — and the "it won't let you break the rules" guardrail quietly does expensive feasibility work for free.*

**8. She moves toward financing — when she's ready.**
Only after she's chosen and configured her home does money enter the picture, and even then it starts as soft estimates before anything formal.
*Why it matters: we lead with desire and save the hard, scary steps for when she's already emotionally bought in. That sequencing is what protects conversion through the funnel.*

The whole arc — from her street view to her configured home — happens **without ever feeling like she changed pages or left her backyard.** It's one continuous, guided experience.

## Why this is hard to copy

- **It works without ever visiting the property.** We build all of this from public mapping data and official city/county property records — so it scales to any address in our markets with zero site visits.
- **It's not just pretty — it's *correct*.** Behind the visuals is a "digital twin" of each lot that knows the real boundaries, the existing house, and the local rules. That's why we can honestly say "this fits" — and that accuracy is our moat. Competitors can show a generic render; we show *your* lot with a *verified* answer.
